Hi Said,

Thank you for the quick and thoughtful reply. Your proposal is exactly how we would like to start: one small internal workflow on your own Odoo instance with demo data, so no client data is involved and we can judge the results quickly.

On your three questions:

1. Demo
Yes, we can show both Knott and Matta on the call, running on demo data. For Knott, we can run an invoice or purchase approval end to end on a local model with nothing leaving the machine: an AI decision step scores its confidence, anything below a set threshold goes to a human reviewer before the ERP is touched, and every step is recorded with its reason in an audit trail. For Matta, we can show how customer, supplier and employee records from different systems become one governed record, with duplicates resolved and every fact traceable to its source system, so the answers Knott's workflows rely on come with evidence attached.

2. Odoo readiness
To be direct: we do not yet have a packaged Odoo connector or ERP mapping, so it would be built during the pilot. Knott connects to other systems through connector definitions and a generic HTTP and GraphQL step, and Odoo exposes an external API, so a first approval or status-update workflow is a small, well-bounded piece of work. Your team's knowledge of Odoo's data model and access rights would make that part faster and more reliable.

3. Terms
Happy to keep this simple at the start. My suggestion: the pilot is at no charge, with each side covering its own time. On ownership, Knott is open source under Apache 2.0 and its core stays with Regnant; anything specific to your Odoo environment or your customers' configurations stays yours; and the Odoo connector we build together would be shared work that we agree on in writing before we start. If the pilot succeeds, we could explore a partner model where Chesify leads Odoo implementation and Regnant provides the platform and support, with the commercial split agreed per client deployment. Matta for consistent master data across Odoo and other systems, and Wallgarden as a decision layer above the ERP, could follow in later phases once the first workflow has proven itself.

To prepare a useful demo, it would help to know your Odoo version and edition (Community or Enterprise, hosted or on-premise), and which workflow you would like to start with, for example purchase approval or a status update.
